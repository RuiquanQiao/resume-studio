# Hiring calendars: what a batch name means, country by country

Read this before you name, read or reason about a role's `batch` (27秋招, 2027 Graduate Program, Summer 2027…) or
about when something opens, closes or starts. The user's batch names are their own words; this is what those words
usually mean, so you can work like someone who knows the market. These are typical windows, gathered in October 2026
from the sources at the end. A company's own page always wins: when a date matters, look it up there.

## The one trap: what the year in a name counts

| Name | The year is… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| China **27届**, 27秋招, 27春招 | the **graduation year** | 27秋招 is the autumn hiring for people graduating in 2027, so it happens in **2026** |
| Japan **27卒** | the graduation year (graduating March 2027) | its selection runs in 2026 |
| Australia, UK, Singapore, HK **2027 Graduate Program / scheme** | the year the job **starts** | applied for in 2026, starts Feb (AU) or Sep (UK) 2027 |
| US **Summer 2027** internship, **2027 New Grad** | the season the internship runs / the year the job starts | Summer 2027 internships are applied for from Aug 2026 |
| Korea 2026 **하반기** 공채 | the calendar half when the hiring happens | autumn 2026 |

So "27" in a Chinese batch and "2027" in an Australian program both mean "applied for in 2026", but for different
reasons. When the user writes a batch, keep their words; when you reason about dates, convert first.

## China (校招)

**届** is the graduation year. Mainland students mostly graduate around June of that year; a delay to July or August
usually still counts. For students abroad, most internet companies count a degree conferred **September of the year
before to August of that year** (27届: 2026-09 to 2027-08), going by the date on the degree certificate, not the end of
classes or the ceremony; some take a wider window (Tencent's 2026 internship call took graduates from 2026-09 to
2027-12). Students abroad have no 三方协议: whether they count as 应届 is each employer's call, so read the posting's
own graduation window.

For 27届 (each step a year earlier for 26届, a year later for 28届):

| Batch | When | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **暑期实习** (27届) | applications Mar–May 2026, internship Jun–Aug 2026 | the main route into a full-time offer (转正); big tech posts thousands of places in March |
| **秋招提前批** | May–Jul 2026 | early round, mostly tech roles at big companies; usually doesn't use up the 正式批 chance |
| **秋招正式批** (27秋招) | Aug–Sep 2026 opening (网申 peak Sep), tests and interviews Sep–Nov, offers Oct–Dec | the biggest round of the year |
| **秋招补录** | Nov–Dec 2026 | filling places left over |
| **27春招** | Feb–Apr 2027 (opens late Jan) | second chance, smaller and faster; also catches people back from 考研/考公 |
| **日常实习** | all year | any time, usually 3–6 months, 4–5 days a week; **寒假实习** is the name for 日常实习 that starts in winter (Dec–Jan), not a batch of its own |
| 签约、入职 | May–Jul 2027 | three-party agreement, then start |

Many roles are 招满即止: the posted end date is a ceiling, not a promise. Apply early.

## Australia

| Batch | Applications | Runs / starts |
|---|---|---|
| **Summer internship / vacation program** (penultimate year) | mostly Feb–Jul, large firms from Feb | late Nov to Feb (Australian summer), 10–12 weeks; often leads straight to a graduate offer |
| **Graduate program** (final year) | biggest wave Feb–Apr, second wave Jul–Sep | starts February of the next year (some July) |
| Big 4 and consulting (MBB) | open Jan–Feb, close late Feb–Mar | |
| **APS** (Australian Government Graduate Program) | about Mar–Apr (2027 intake: 2 Mar–27 Apr 2026) | Feb 2027; citizens only |

"2027 Graduate Program" means starting in 2027, applied for in 2026. Most roles go through the employer's site or
SEEK Grad (formerly GradConnection) and Prosple.

## United States

| Batch | Applications | Runs / starts |
|---|---|---|
| **Summer internship** (Summer 2027) | big tech opens Jul–Sep 2026, most postings Aug–Nov, interviews Oct–Mar | Jun–Aug 2027 |
| **New grad** full time (2027 New Grad) | Aug–Dec of the year before, a smaller spring wave | after graduation, mostly summer/fall 2027 |

Almost everyone reviews on a rolling basis: the first weeks after a portal opens count most. Return offers from the
summer internship fill much of the new-grad class.

## Canada

Co-op terms are Winter (Jan), Summer (May) and Fall (Sep). Hiring for a term happens in the term before: Summer jobs
are applied for Jan–Mar, Fall jobs May–Jul. New-grad hiring looks like the US's.

## United Kingdom

| Batch | Applications | Runs / starts |
|---|---|---|
| **Spring week / insight** (first year) | Sep–Oct of the year before | Mar–Apr |
| **Summer internship** (penultimate year) | from Aug, most close Oct–Nov | 10–12 weeks the next summer |
| **Graduate scheme** (final year) | from Aug, most close Oct–Dec | Sep of the next year |
| **Placement year** (industrial placement) | Sep–Jan | a year in industry before the final year |

Rolling review again: banks and consultancies often close before their stated deadline.

## Japan (新卒一括採用)

27卒 = graduating March 2027. Government rules for 27卒: information opens **1 March 2026**, selection from **1 June
2026**, formal offers (内定) from **1 October 2026**. In practice selection starts earlier through summer and winter
internships in the third year (2025), and specialist roles may be selected from internships. Foreign graduates can
apply through the same 新卒 route.

## South Korea (공채 / 수시채용)

Large groups traditionally run two open rounds: **상반기** (Mar–Apr) and **하반기** (Sep–Oct, usually larger), with
written aptitude tests (e.g. GSAT) and several interviews. Many (Hyundai, Kia, LG…) have moved to **수시채용**,
year-round hiring per team, and to 채용연계형 인턴 (internships that convert).

## Singapore and Hong Kong

Graduate programmes and **management trainee (MT)** programmes mostly open Aug–Sep and close Oct–Nov (Hang Seng MT
2027: 8 Sep–30 Nov 2026), starting the next summer. Banks review on a rolling basis.

## India

Campus placements run inside the university: summer-internship placement around Nov–Feb for the following May–Jul;
final placements start with pre-placement talks Sep–Nov and placement week in early December. A good internship can
end in a pre-placement offer (PPO).

## Germany, the Netherlands, France and much of continental Europe

Mostly no season: graduate entry (Direkteinstieg), trainee programmes (often starting in spring or autumn), Werkstudent
jobs while studying and internships are posted all year, each with its own start date. France's stage de fin d'études
(up to six months) is usually found three to six months before it starts; alternance follows the academic year.

## How to use this

- **Naming a batch**: reuse the names the user's roles already have (`list`). When there are none yet, use the
  market's own word for the round in the language of the posting (27秋招, 2027 Graduate Program, Summer 2027 Internship),
  never a scheme of your own.
- **Reading a batch**: convert it to dates before you advise on timing ("27春招" is Feb–Apr 2027).
- **Eligibility**: check the posting's graduation window against the user's graduation date in the profile notes,
  especially for students abroad applying in China.
- **When it matters, look it up**: these windows shift by a few weeks each year and differ by company. Search the
  company's own page (or the posting) and give the source.
